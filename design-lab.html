Flat Design Sheet

One language: solid fills, 1px borders, no shadows on resting surfaces, no gradients, no glows. Depth budget = a single --shadow-float reserved for floating layers (nav, dropdowns, modals). Hover = color shift; press = deepen + scale(0.97). This page renders the real shared classes under a page-scoped override of src/site.css — what you see is what ships.

Buttons

Solid fill + 1px border. Hover = color shift only; press = deepen + scale(0.97). Focus = crisp accent ring.

Badges & segmented

Bordered flat chips, same recipe as the buttons. Selected segment = solid accent, white text.

Checks & radios

Unchecked = neutral chip; checked = solid accent + white glyph. Focus ring kept.

Inputs

White fields, 1px border, no inset recessed shadow. Focus = accent border + flat ring.
