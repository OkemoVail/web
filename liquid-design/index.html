Liquid Design

Glass that stretches. Add one attribute to a button you already have, and it becomes a piece of liquid glass that blurs what is behind it, bends under your finger and springs back.

Press, hold and drag past the edge. The glass stretches along one axis and rebounds when you let go.

Liquid Design is a small runtime: one stylesheet and one script, no dependencies and no build step. It never replaces your markup. Your button keeps its element, its click handler, its focus ring and its layout box. The engine paints the material behind the content and owns only the decoration it adds.

  • Opt in, per controlNothing changes until you add data-liquid-design.
  • Native behaviourLinks navigate, forms submit, inputs type. Hitboxes never move.
  • Accessible by defaultRespects reduced motion, reduced transparency, more contrast and forced colours.
Note Liquid Design is a browser approximation inspired by Apple's Liquid Glass, not Apple's renderer.

Installation

Copy the liquid-design/ folder into your site. At runtime you only need two files: liquid-design.css and liquid-design.js. Load the stylesheet first, then the script with defer.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="liquid-design/liquid-design.css">
<script defer src="liquid-design/liquid-design.js"></script>

The script initialises itself on DOMContentLoaded and exposes a global LiquidDesign object. There is nothing to configure.

Build from source The modules live in liquid-design/source/. Run node liquid-design/build.mjs to rebuild the bundle.

Quick start

Style your button the way you normally would. The glass takes its shape from your button's size and border-radius, so a pill stays a pill and a circle stays a circle.

The engine removes your button's own background and border colour while it is enhanced, and puts them back if you call LiquidDesign.destroy().

Buttons and links

Any <button> or <a> can be glass. Icon buttons work the same way: give them a fixed size and a 50% radius.

Go to surfaces
Siblings share glass automatically When two or more enhanced controls sit in the same parent, the engine treats them as one group with one shared material. Add data-liquid-design-independent to keep each one separate, as above.

A drag that moves more than a couple of pixels is treated as a stretch, not a click, so pulling on a button never fires it by accident. Keyboard activation with Enter and Space is unchanged.

Surfaces and forms

Use data-liquid-design="surface" for larger glass that holds editable content: search bars, composers, panels. Inputs and textareas keep native typing, selection and caret. Surfaces keep a light veil so text on them stays readable, and they stretch with more resistance than small buttons.

Buttons nested inside a surface get their own glass and stretch on their own. If you want a press on any nested row to stretch the whole panel instead, add data-liquid-design-static-actions to the surface. A still release still clicks the row; a pull does not.

Shared glass groups

A toolbar reads best as one piece of glass. Put data-liquid-design-group on the container and every enhanced control inside joins it. Each button still stretches on its own; their shapes melt together where they touch.

data-liquid-design-independent on a single control overrides both the explicit group and the automatic sibling grouping.

Disabled controls

There are two kinds of disabled. Use aria-disabled="true" when the control should stay focusable and still feel alive: it stretches, dims its content to 50% and swallows clicks and Enter/Space. The native disabled attribute removes it from interaction entirely, including the glass motion.

Options menu

The trigger morphs into the menu: one piece of glass grows out of the button, the rows fade in sharp, and closing reverses the same path back into the button. Click it, or hold for 260 ms and drag onto a row, then release to choose.

Structure

  • A wrapper with data-liquid-design-component="options".
  • A trigger with data-liquid-design-toggle set to the panel's id.
  • The panel inside the wrapper, starting hidden inert, with one enhanced button per row.

Arrow keys, Home and End move between rows, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger, and a click outside dismisses. The menu opens downward and flips up near the bottom of the viewport. Force a direction with data-liquid-design-menu-direction="up" or "down" on the wrapper.

Tools

Tools splits a row of actions out of its trigger like droplets, and absorbs them back on the second click. Actions open towards the side with more room. The markup matches Options, with "tools" instead.

Actions are 52 px circles by default. Size them with your own CSS on the action buttons, and set the spacing with --liquid-design-tools-gap (a unitless pixel value, default 9).

Coordinated navigation

When an Options menu and a Tools row share a bar, wrap both in data-liquid-design-navigation and put data-liquid-design-move on the positioned wrapper around Options. The engine then keeps them out of each other's way:

  • Opening Options closes Tools.
  • If the menu would cover the Tools trigger, it opens below it with a 12 px gap (--liquid-design-navigation-gap).
  • If expanded Tools would cover Options, the Options wrapper slides off the left edge, and returns when Tools closes.

Your CSS still owns where both wrappers sit. Controls never shrink to make room.

Theme

Glass follows the operating system's light or dark preference. To follow your site's own theme switch instead, set data-liquid-design-theme on <html> or on any ancestor. Changes apply immediately.

// Your theme toggle
document.documentElement.dataset.liquidDesignTheme = isDark ? 'dark' : 'light';

The engine also treats an ancestor with the class dark as dark. This page uses the attribute: try the theme button at the top.

Tint and ink

Buttons are clear by default: no veil, only blur, refraction and an edge of light. Give a button colour for a primary action with --liquid-design-tint, and set its text and icon colour with --liquid-design-color. Use a translucent tint so the glass still shows through.

Fill is for surfaces --liquid-design-fill changes the veil on surfaces and open menus. Clear buttons ignore it; use a tint.

Nearby colour

Real glass picks up colour from bright objects next to it. Mark an element with data-liquid-color-source and glass within 80 px catches its colour on the facing rim, with a faint wash inside. Leave the value empty to use the element's background colour, or give a colour.

Widen the reach for one piece of glass with --liquid-design-color-range (pixels, default 80).

Refraction

      
In Chromium browsers the glass bends what is behind it. The middle stays flat; the edge rolls off like a thick slab of glass, so stripes and text curve as they reach the rim. The bend comes from a displacement map generated per shape and cached, so it costs nothing while you are not changing size.

      

        


        

      

      
    
        
  • Turn it off for one subtree with data-liquid-design-refraction="off", or for the page by setting it on <html>.
    • 
        
  • Scale it with --liquid-design-refraction.
    • 
        
  • Debug it with <html data-liquid-design-refraction-debug>: each lens shows its map (red is sideways pull, green is vertical, grey is no bend).
    • 
      

      

      
Safari and iOS WebKit cannot feed an SVG filter into backdrop-filter, so there the glass keeps blur and its edge light without the bend. Nothing breaks.

    


    
    

      
Backdrop adaptation

      
Clear glass is only as readable as what is behind it. The engine samples a small grid under each control (page colours, same-origin images and CSS backgrounds) and sets data-lgp-backdrop="dark|light" on it. Over dark content the veil lifts and the ink turns light when your theme's ink would clash; over light content a clear button gains a thin veil and a firmer contour. Changes fade over 220 ms.

      

        



        

      

      
    
        
  • Opt out with data-liquid-design-adapt="off" on a control, a subtree or <html>.
    • 
        
  • Controls pinned on one line within 48 px pick one ink together. Force a cluster with a shared data-liquid-design-adapt-group="name".
    • 
        
  • Fixed overlays that do not take pointer events are composited into the reading. Mark other overlays with data-liquid-design-scrim, or exclude one with ="off".
    • 
      

      
Cross-origin images cannot be read, so they are skipped. If glass sits over one, set an explicit ink with --liquid-design-color.

    


    
    

      
JavaScript API

      
The engine enhances what is in the page at load. For content you add later, call refresh. All three methods take an optional scope element and default to the whole document.

      

        
MethodWhat it does

        
          
LiquidDesign.refresh(scope?)Enhances new controls in scope and re-measures existing ones.

          
LiquidDesign.destroy(scope?)Removes the glass, listeners and springs, and restores the original styles and attributes.

          
LiquidDesign.repaint(scope?)Repaints shapes without re-enhancing. Use during your own CSS transitions.

        
      

      
const card = document.createElement('div');
card.innerHTML = '<button data-liquid-design>New action</button>';
list.append(card);
LiquidDesign.refresh(card);

// Later, before removing a whole view
LiquidDesign.destroy(view);

      

        


        

        

          
          
        

      

      
Removed controls are cleaned up on their own, so you only need destroy to turn the glass off while keeping the elements.

Accessibility

      
    
        
  • Semantics stay yours. Roles, names, focus order and keyboard activation are your element's. Decoration is aria-hidden and ignores pointer events.
    • 
        
  • Reduced motion turns off stretching, springs and menu morphs; menus open and close instantly. The setting is watched live.
    • 
        
  • Reduced transparency and more contrast give the glass an opaque fill with no blur, refraction or lens layer.
    • 
        
  • Forced colours use system colours.
    • 
        
  • Hitboxes never move. The material stretches; your layout box does not.
    • 
        
  • Text selection is off on glass labels so a drag never selects them, and stays on inside inputs and editable content.
    • 
      

      
Keep your own visible :focus-visible style on buttons. Surfaces drop the extra focus box because the field inside shows focus.

      
Testing Under WebDriver the engine keeps controls still and skips refraction and sampling, so snapshots are deterministic. Opt back in with data-liquid-design-refraction="on" and data-liquid-design-adapt="on" on <html>.
