Your controls. Liquid Design.

These are ordinary buttons, links, and a form. Each enhanced control has one data-liquid-design attribute. Hold and pull to stretch; release to rebound. Drag the colored objects in each box near the glass to tint its edge. Focus an object and use arrow keys to move it.
